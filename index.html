FAQ

Questions before you start?

Is Skill Exchange free?

Yes. The basic Skill Exchange section is designed around peer-to-peer exchange, so you teach a skill and learn another skill in return.

What is LearnLoop Pro?

Pro is a paid learning option for selected advanced programs that require more structured curriculum, projects and professional guidance. The planned price is ₹499 per month.

Do I need experience to join?

No. Select your current level in the registration form. Beginner learners are welcome.

Will I receive a confirmation email?

Yes. Once the email service is configured on Render, successful registrations receive a confirmation email containing their LearnLoop registration ID and selected program.

Does the ₹499 payment work automatically?

This version includes the Pro plan and pricing interface. Actual subscription payments require a payment gateway such as Stripe or Razorpay to be connected separately.
